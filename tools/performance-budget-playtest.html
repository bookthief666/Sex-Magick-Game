1 · Run one controlled repeat

Choose a preset. The game opens in a same-origin tab; play for at least 60 seconds after warm-up, return here, then capture.

  1. Physically use the matching closed or open posture.
  2. Play normally for at least 60 seconds after the probe warm-up.
  3. Avoid app switching, notification shades, split screen, and system overlays.
  4. Return to this tab and use Capture active run. The capture stops only the probe, not the game.
  5. Collect at least three eligible repeats for all six presets. Five repeats improves confidence.

Interpretation boundary

The gate may return insufficient-evidence or no-sustainable-candidate. Those are valid outcomes. The analysis does not authenticate device identity, prove untampered evidence, measure GPU memory, thermal throttling, battery cost, subjective clarity, Samsung Internet, Safari, broad hardware support, fun, or release readiness. The FNV fingerprint is for duplicate detection only, not security.
